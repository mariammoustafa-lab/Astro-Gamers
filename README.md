# 🌌 Astro Gamers - Space Relics

> **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 Project**  
> *Transforming space education into an interactive, voiceover-narrated gaming experience for young explorers.*

---

## 📖 About the Project
**Astro Gamers** is an interactive online educational web game developed by team **Astro Gamers**. Our project tackles the challenge of teaching children about the historical rovers, scientific instruments, and equipment left behind by NASA on the Moon and Mars. 

Instead of presenting dry, text-heavy articles, we gamified the learning process. By combining engaging gameplay mechanics with professional audio narration, our project makes NASA’s space exploration history accessible, entertaining, and easy to grasp for young learners—even those who cannot yet read.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **📖 Immersive Gamification:** Turning real NASA mission history and space exploration into a fun, interactive game.
* **🎙️ Integrated Voiceover:** Built-in audio narration that speaks directly to users, ensuring accessibility for younger children.
* **🔭 Educational Value:** Teaches children about actual lunar and Martian rovers and equipment left by NASA.
* **📱 Responsive Design:** Built as a web application optimized for smooth performance across devices.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React.js, HTML5, SCSS, React-Scrollama
* **Styling & Fonts:** Google Fonts (Poppins), Mobile-First Responsive Layout
* **Deployment:** Vercel / Netlify

---

## 🤖 Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Tools
To accelerate development, structure content, and enhance user experience, our team utilized the following AI and digital tools:
* **Nano Banana** & **Pixaverse** (Visual and asset generation/styling support)
* **ChatGPT** & **Gemini** (Text structuring, project descriptions, and concept drafting)
* **Narakeet** (Voiceover and audio generation support)
* **Claude (Anthropic):** Utilized as a primary coding and debugging assistant to refactor logic, resolve technical bottlenecks, and optimize the React.js components.
*(Note: All AI-generated assets adhere to the challenge guidelines, ensuring proper transparency, watermarking where required, and zero misuse of official NASA branding.)*
